VYBZ Date Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Introduction

VYBZ Date is a dating and social networking application for adults. This policy explains how we handle personal information when users use the app.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, information may include account details, profile information, photos, messages, preferences, and information you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, enable matching and communication, provide app features, maintain security, and improve the service.

Sharing Information

Information may be visible to other users according to the app's features and your settings. We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the app or comply with law.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may contact the app developer to ask questions or request deletion of their personal information. Requests will be handled subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

VYBZ Date is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not intended for children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the developer through the contact details provided in the app's Google Play listing.
